# Abdulazez Abagero

I’m an aerospace engineering researcher with an MSc in Aerospace Engineering. My work spans autonomous UAV navigation, reinforcement learning, CubeSat power systems, and aerodynamic optimization.

## Research Demonstrations

- **UAV reinforcement learning in Isaac Lab** — A simulation demonstration of policy training for autonomous UAV navigation. [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GY_i5F9RiYg)
- **Crazyflie swarm flight** — An indoor demonstration of coordinated flight with multiple Crazyflie drones. [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/aovQiCbUqWQ)

## Selected Publications

- **[Deep Learning-Based MPPT Approach to Enhance CubeSat Power Generation](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3546066)** · *IEEE Access*, 2025. First-author study on using a neural network for maximum power point tracking in a simulated CubeSat solar power system.
- **[Accelerated Airfoil Optimization via a Geometry-Based Discriminator Within Particle Swarm Optimization](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3683048)** · *IEEE Access*, 2026. Co-authored study on using geometry-based filtering to reduce aerodynamic solver calls during airfoil optimization.
